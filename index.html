Our story

A little café with a serious love of coffee.

Parkgate is a small, independent coffee house on Prince's Street in the heart of Tralee. We pour speciality coffee with beans from Irish roasters Roasted Brown, and bake fresh every morning — from millionaire's shortbread to a proper pear & almond tart.

Grab a flat white on your way past, settle in with a breakfast pot, or take a little home — we stock brewing kit and beans too, from AeroPress and Bialetti to KeepCup, Chilly's bottles and Urney chocolates.

  • ✺ Speciality beans by Roasted Brown
  • ✺ Fresh bakes & gluten-free options
  • ✺ Plant-based always · 20c off with a clean KeepCup
