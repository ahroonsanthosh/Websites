Our story

A little café with a serious love of coffee.

Parkgate began with a simple idea: that a great cup of coffee can set the tone for the whole day. We roast in small batches, pour over with patience, and bake everything fresh each morning in our Tralee kitchen.

Whether you're grabbing a flat white on the way to work or settling in for a slow Sunday brunch, you'll find the same thing every time — proper coffee, good food, and the kind of welcome Kerry is known for.

  • ✺ Small-batch house roast
  • ✺ Locally sourced milk & bakes
  • ✺ Plant-based options always
