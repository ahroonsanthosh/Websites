Our Story

A neighbourhood cafe with soul

Nestled on Cork's beloved Oliver Plunkett Street, 115 Cafe is a place to slow down, savour, and connect. We source quality ingredients, brew exceptional coffee, and serve food that feels like home — whether you're grabbing a quick flat white or settling in for a long lunch.

From early risers to evening gatherings, our doors are open for Cork city and everyone passing through it.
