Oliver Plunkett St

Oliver Plunkett Street

Bold red cups.
Warm Cork welcome.

Step inside to find red leather sofas, exposed stone walls and floor-to-ceiling windows flooding the room with light from the street. 115 is unmistakably Cork — vivid, warm, and always buzzing.

By day we serve exceptional coffee in our signature red cups alongside fresh food made to order. Come Thursday evening, the lights soften and the menu extends late into the night.
