Our Story

A bright little brunch room in Tralee.

Tucked inside Boyles of Ballyseedy, Buttermilk is where Kerry mornings slow down. We cook proper brunch all day long — fluffy homemade buttermilk pancakes, free-range eggs, a Full Irish worth the trip, and a salad bar that changes with the season.

Everything is made by hand using local producers we love — Matt's sausages, Burke's puddings, Garyhinch wild mushrooms and Fingal Ferguson chorizo. Good food, made with good people, for good times.
