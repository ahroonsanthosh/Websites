01 — Our Story

Some people wait to feel ready. Others step on the mat.

Dragon Jiu-Jitsu Academy has been Ballincollig's grappling home since 2014. We built this gym on a simple idea: real technique, taught patiently, in a room where everyone — from nervous first-timers to seasoned competitors — is made to feel welcome.

You'll be coached by people who still compete and still learn. Every class is structured, supervised and built to make you better, safer and sharper each week.

03 — Why Dragon

  • Coaching you can trust
  • A real community
  • Proven on the mats

Every session is led personally by a black belt. Technique first, safety always — you're never just a number on a packed mat.

Ballincollig locals, students, shift-workers and families train side by side. You'll leave your first class with new friends and a reason to come back.

Our team competes and wins — from local opens to the IBJJF Milan International Open. The same game we teach you is tested at the highest level.

Black Belt · Head Coach

04 — Your Coach

Piotr

Founder & Head Coach

Piotr founded Dragon in 2014 and still leads the room himself. A competitive black belt, he coaches with the patience of a teacher and the eye of an athlete — breaking the art down so anyone can learn it, then building it back up for those chasing the podium.

Whether it's your very first class or your next competition, you'll be learning from someone who lives it every single day.
