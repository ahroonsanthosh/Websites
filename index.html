Our Story

French soul,
Kerry welcome

Chez Christophe brings the warmth of a French bistro to the heart of Tralee. From a rich beef bourguignon to a delicate pan-fried turbot, every plate is cooked with classic technique and the best of local Kerry produce.

And when the mood calls for something simpler, our traditional wood-fired oven turns out crisp, smoky pizza — authentic taste, made fresh and cooked hot, just for you.
