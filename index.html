Our story

Good food, good coffee, under the white branches.

Tucked on the corner of The Mall, Wild is Tralee's bright spot for a proper start to the day. Step in off the street and you're under our signature canopy of white branches and warm Edison light — the kind of room you settle into and lose an hour in.

We cook free-range, bake our own brown bread, and pour coffee worth slowing down for. Breakfast, brunch, lunch or just a flat white to go — there's a seat with your name on it.
