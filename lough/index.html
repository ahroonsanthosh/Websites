Cork's front room, by the water

Strong coffee, odd chairs, a lake out the window.

The Lough Café is Cork's most lived-in living room — secondhand armchairs, a wall of borrowed art, proper coffee, and a dog who has opinions about your table. Two minutes from the water at The Lough.

How we got so cluttered

Every chair has a different story.

Nothing in this building was bought to match anything else. The chairs came from a dozen different houses, the art came from customers who never came back for it, and the bookshelves grew the way bookshelves do — one spine at a time, over years.

We didn't design a café. We just kept the good stuff we found, put a decent espresso machine behind the counter, and left the door open so the regulars — two legged and four — could let themselves in.

— pulled up a chair yet?

The other reason people stay

Across the road is the actual lake.

"The Lough" isn't a brand name — it's the lake and park fifty steps from our door. Coffee on the deck, then a lap around the water. That's the whole plan.
