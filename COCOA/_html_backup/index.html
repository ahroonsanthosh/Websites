Our Story

A Labour
of Love

Tucked away on Winthrop Street in the heart of Cork City, COCOA was born from a deep passion for fine chocolate and the belief that every cup, every truffle, every pastry should be something worth pausing for.

We source the finest cacao and craft each chocolate by hand — from single-origin bars to silk-smooth drinking chocolate, from seasonal truffles to gift boxes made to delight. Pull up a chair beneath our Edison lights, and let the city wait a while.
